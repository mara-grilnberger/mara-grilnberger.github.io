About me

I am a PhD student in the Big Data Algorithms Group advised by Sebastian Forster at the University of Salzburg, where I previously obtained my master's degree in Computer Science.

My reserach interests lie in theoretical computer science, specifically graph algorithms and related combinatorial optimization problems. I am currently especially interested in algorithms in the dynamic setting that maintain a solution under changing input.
